Google Drive Backup

SplitMate can back up your expense data to your own Google Drive account. You can create a backup from inside the app and restore your data whenever you need it.

Your data, your Google account. SplitMate uses Google Drive for its application-specific backup data. It does not need access to unrelated files in your Drive.
